## Brand & Style

This design system drives a next-generation agri-aquaculture digital marketplace combining technical precision, agronomic expertise, and frictionless commercial transactions. The aesthetic fuses **Modern Corporate** clarity with **Contemporary Tactile Minimalism**, engineered to establish absolute trust among modern commercial farmers, hydroponic entrepreneurs, and aquaculture hobbyists across Indonesia.

The emotional goal is empowerment, reliability, and biological prosperity:
- **Trust & Authority:** Structured layouts and crisp visual tokens give confidence in high-ticket smart farming IoT solutions, pond kits, and premium bio-inputs.
- **Organic Vitality:** Deep forest greens and botanical emeralds reflect vigorous crop health and crystal aquaculture waters, accented by warm gold/amber sun tones that indicate harvest readiness and prime commercial value.
- **Conversion Optimization:** High legibility, micro-interactions, subtle glassmorphism badges, and distinct calls-to-action minimize cognitive fatigue during inventory browsing and technical checkout.

## Colors

The color palette centers around a refined ecological spectrum calibrated for optimal digital contrast:

- **Primary (`#1B4D3E` — Deep Forest Emerald):** The foundational anchor representing biological growth, ecological sustainability, and stable infrastructure. Used for primary navigation surfaces, high-prominence headers, hero backgrounds, and primary action buttons.
- **Secondary (`#D99B26` — Warm Sunlit Amber):** Evoking golden harvest, energy, and premium value. Applied to secondary conversion actions, key highlight tags, active promotional accents, and high-urgency notifications.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` — Vibrant Mint Leaf):** A digital-native biological accent signaling active connectivity, operational IoT telemetry, healthy growth metrics, and stock availability badges.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B` — Slate Obsidian):** Deep slate-tinted charcoal for high-contrast typography and icon glyphs, replacing harsh pure black with balanced readability against warm off-white canvases (`#F8FAF8`).

### Color Applications
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAF8` for page surfaces, `#FFFFFF` for primary elevated cards.
- **Dark Surface Shells:** `#13382D` to `#1B4D3E` for headers, promotional banners, and global footers.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle botanical grey `#E2E8F0` at 80% opacity, preventing visual fragmentation.
- **Status Tones:** Success (`#15803D`), Discount/Destructive (`#E11D48`), Information/Sensor (`#0284C7`).

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies. Its geometric construction, humanized terminals, and high x-height deliver maximum technical precision alongside an approachable tone suited for Indonesian agricultural trade.

- **Display & Headings:** Formed with tighter negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) and bold-to-extrabold weights for punchy messaging and immediate visual hierarchy in hero banners and category headers.
- **Numbers & Monetary Figures:** Currency values (`Rp`) use tabular numeric alignment and medium-to-bold weights to facilitate rapid mental comparisons within dense product catalogs.
- **Body & Microcopy:** Clean geometry guarantees readability across dense product manuals, specification sheets, hydroponic dosing guides, and order receipts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt base grid with a 12-column fluid desktop structure maxing out at `1280px` to maintain strict focus and readable line lengths.

### Form Factor Behavior
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) margins. Product listings render in 4-column configurations; category pills support horizontal overflow or 6-item single-row distribution.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1.5rem` (24px) margins. Product catalogs adapt to a 3-column or 2-column responsive layout.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `0.75rem` (12px) gutters, `1rem` (16px) margins. Product cards format into a 2-column continuous scroll; category cards shift into a frictionless touch-snapped horizontal rail.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through subtle, light-permeable layering that balances organic softness with digital crispness:

- **Ambient Shading:** Avoid heavy, murky dropshadows. All elevations utilize an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with deep forest tone (`rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.06)` to `rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.12)`) blended with a minimal ambient slate blur.
- **Flat Card Surface with Boundary Line:** Static cards rest on a pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` continuous border in `#E2E8F0`. Hover transitions slightly elevate the card (`translateY(-3px)`) and increase shadow spread while darkening the border slightly to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Glassmorphic Floating Tiers:** Used for contextual badges (e.g., “Unggulan”, “Diskon”), floating bottom bars, and active category pills overlaid on rich photography. Standard implementation: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`, with a crisp `1px` translucent border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`).
- **Hero Stacking:** Hero canvas containers deploy a soft internal gradient from `#13382D` to `#1B4D3E`, providing deep atmospheric depth behind high-contrast golden headline copy.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded (`2`)** geometry archetype (`0.5rem` / `8px` base curve, scaling to `1rem` / `16px` for structural modules and `1.5rem` / `24px` for hero wrappers and large banners).

This balance softens technological jargon and heavy agricultural machinery, projecting approachable modern engineering while retaining structural stability for e-commerce conversion blocks. Mini badges, category chips, and search bars utilize fully rounded pill contours to create inviting interactive touch targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Styled in `#1B4D3E` with crisp white text, or `#D99B26` with deep green/slate text for high-urgency hero actions. Features `rounded-md` (8px) or `rounded-full` (for pills), `padding: 0.75rem 1.5rem`, tracking label `label-lg`, and an subtle hover brightness shift with an internal micro-glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost CTA:** High-contrast outlined button with `1.5px` border in `#1B4D3E`, transparent backdrop, and text in `#1B4D3E`. On dark hero banners, border and text switch to `#FFFFFF` with glassmorphic hover fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Icon Quick-Cart Button:** Compact rounded action button (`36px × 36px` or `40px × 40px`) situated on the bottom right of product cards. Default state: `#1B4D3E` fill with white cart icon; active/hover scales 1.05 with amber accent fill `#D99B26`.

### Cards
- **Product Card:** Pure white container with `rounded-lg` (16px), subtle border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, and overflow hidden. Image container features a `1:1` aspect ratio on neutral light tint (`#F1F5F2`), complete with absolute-positioned floating glassmorphism tags on the top corners. Card body houses sub-category meta, bold 2-line title, tabular price (`price-md`), and integrated cart action aligned baseline.
- **Category Nav Card:** Vertical orientation, soft centered icon enclosed in an organic circular badge (`48px × 48px`, background `rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.06)`), title in `label-lg`, and subtext in `body-sm`. Hover introduces a subtle border coloration to `#10B981`.

### Badges & Chips
- **Status/Promo Badges:** Pill-shaped tags (`rounded-full`) with `padding: 0.25rem 0.625rem`. 
  - *Unggulan:* Glassmorphic frosted amber backing with deep amber label (`#92400E`).
  - *Diskon:* Vibrant ruby red backdrop (`#FEE2E2`) with `#B91C1C` text.
  - *Smart IoT:* Mint emerald translucent backing (`#D1FAE5`) with `#065F46` label.

### Form Inputs & Search Bar
- **Global Search Bar:** Elongated pill field (`rounded-full`), padded with left-mounted search glyph and right-mounted quick-clear / category dropdown selector. Canvas: `#FFFFFF` or tinted neutral `#F8FAF8`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, focusing seamlessly to `2px solid #1B4D3E` without harsh outline jumps.
- **Form Controls:** Checkboxes and radio buttons feature a deep emerald `#1B4D3E` active fill with smooth scale transitions, adhering to standard accessible 20px hitboxes.

### Trust & Value Highlights
- Horizontal strip positioned below heroes, presenting 4 pillar items (Pengiriman Cepat, Garansi 100%, Konsultasi Ahli, Transaksi Aman). Each feature highlights a micro-monoline vector icon inside a soft green circle with clear dual-line typography.
